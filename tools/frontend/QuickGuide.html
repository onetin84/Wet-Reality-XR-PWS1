Room-scale VR for PowerWash Simulator.
The washer follows your hand in six degrees of freedom — where you look and where you spray are fully independent.
Start the game through Steam as usual — that is the whole procedure. Put the headset on: VR comes up in the main menu, and the washer is in your hand as soon as a level has loaded. To quit, use the game’s own menu.

Have your headset software (for example Virtual Desktop) running before you start the game.

If something looks wrong

The washer does not sit in my handCalibrate in game (free hand: grip + X + Y) or use the grip fine-tuning in the configurator
Menus too big or too closeMenu size and distance in the configurator
A red line comes out of the nozzleThat is the aiming laser — switch it off in the configurator
VR did not startStart the headset software first, then the game. MelonLoader\Latest.log says what the mod found

The first launch after installing MelonLoader takes a while with no sign of progress: it generates its support files once. It has not crashed — let it finish.
